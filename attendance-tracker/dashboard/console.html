Compliance & notice

No activity is collected for an employee/device pair until the on-device notice has been shown and acknowledged by that employee. This is enforced by the collection pipeline, not just by policy.

What's collected: foreground application, active browser tab/domain, keyboard & mouse timing (never keystroke content), idle and lock state. Never collected: passwords, clipboard contents, message contents, screenshots, camera or microphone.

Corrections log

Every manual correction preserves the original value, the corrected value, the reason, the admin who made it, and when — nothing is silently overwritten.

Correction requests

Requests submitted by employees from their own records. An employee can never alter raw data directly — approving a request here is what actually creates an entry in the corrections log; rejecting one leaves all data untouched.
